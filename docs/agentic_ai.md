# Agentic and multi-agent authority — v1.3.0

RDFR-CI treats delegation as a new authority event. A parent may create sandboxed specialists without granting effectful tools. Privilege-bearing activation requires the child's own local risk assessment, its local gates, explicit scope, and capability intersection.

The numerical rule is Equation (12). The capability intersection is:

`parent_delegable ∩ child_requested ∩ policy_allowed`.

Every privilege-bearing handoff should be independently recorded with sender, recipient, requested and allowed tools, evidence basis, approval, expiry/revocation state, and resulting change. The minimum rule limits a single delegated action; it does not bound cumulative trajectory harm across many individually permitted actions.
